Executive Summary

The Cricut AutoPress 2 15x15 is a compelling upgrade for frequent heat-transfer crafters and small home businesses that need repeatable pressure, full-shirt coverage, and hands-free pressing without moving into industrial equipment. It expands the heat plate to 15 by 15 inches, adds adjustable pressure from roughly 45 to 220 pounds, reaches 410°F, and uses a full slide-out tray that makes garment positioning easier than working beneath a hot clamshell plate.

The strongest market signal is timing: Cricut launched the press July 28, 2026 as an Amazon-exclusive U.S. product for its first 90 days, and exact-ASIN tracking already shows early owner ratings.

This is not a hands-on QuietTrends test. Cricut's documentation establishes size, pressure, temperature, material clearance, controls, and electrical requirements. Independent hands-on testing supports the usefulness of adjustable pressure and the slide-out tray. The biggest ownership constraint is physical: the machine weighs about 41.9 pounds and needs roughly 36.5 inches of front-to-back workspace with the tray fully extended.

Product Deep Dive

Adjustable Pressure Is the Upgrade That Changes the Machine

The original AutoPress applied essentially fixed pressure. AutoPress 2 lets users select pressure from roughly 45 to 220 pounds, expanding the range of transfer types it can handle confidently.

  • DTF transfers often depend on enough pressure to establish a durable bond.
  • Puff iron-on is especially pressure-sensitive.
  • Four customized presets speed repeat jobs.

Buyer takeaway: Adjustable pressure is the main reason this model is more useful than the original for modern transfer workflows.

The 15-by-15 Plate Fits Full-Size Designs Better

Cricut expanded the working area from the original model's 15-by-12 plate to a square 15-by-15 ceramic-coated plate. That extra depth reduces overlapping press cycles on larger adult shirts, sweatshirts, totes, and transfer sheets.

The Slide-Out Tray Improves Access — and Requires Space

AutoPress 2 uses a full slide-out lower tray, making the complete pressing surface visible during setup. The closed machine is roughly 21.0 inches wide by 16.2 inches deep by 11.6 inches high; with the tray fully extended, front-to-back depth grows to about 36.5 inches.

Forty-Two Pounds Makes This a Workstation

Cricut lists the machine at 41.9 pounds. That helps stability but means this is not something most users will casually pull from a closet for one shirt and put away again.

Buyer takeaway: Plan a permanent craft table, direct grounded outlet, and lifting route before delivery.

Bluetooth Is Optional, Which Is the Right Choice

AutoPress 2 can receive time, temperature, and pressure settings from Cricut Design Space on a compatible mobile device, but the included control pod also supports direct operation.

410°F and 1.8-Inch Clearance Cover Most Flat Projects

The machine supports common HTV, DTF, sublimation, Infusible Ink, puff materials, and many thicker flat blanks. Hats, mugs, tumblers, and curved items still need dedicated equipment.

Real-World Ownership Check

  • Workspace: Measure the tray fully extended, not only the closed machine.
  • Power: Plan for a direct grounded outlet rather than routine extension-cord use.
  • Heat: Keep children, pets, loose fabric, and heat-sensitive storage away from the operating area.
  • Batch workflow: Saved presets help, but different garment and transfer brands still need test presses.
  • Storage: Treat this model as a fixed workstation tool.
  • Long-term evidence: The exact generation is only weeks old, so heating-element, tray, hinge, and control-pod durability remain developing.
