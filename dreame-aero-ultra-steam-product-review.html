Executive Summary

Consider the Dreame Aero Ultra Steam if your home has extensive sealed hard flooring and you want vacuuming, hot-water washing, steam treatment, and foam cleaning in one cordless machine. Wait if you need proven stain removal, reliable edge cleaning, easy parts support, or evidence that its ambitious self-cleaning system stays fresh over time.

The pitch is luxurious simplicity. The catch is that nearly every impressive number still comes from the manufacturer.

This is a newly launched flagship, not an established recommendation. Its design appears thoughtful and its cleaning modes are unusually broad, but there is not yet enough independent testing or owner history to know whether the extra complexity earns its premium price.

Skip If

A premium floor washer should reduce household friction, not merely add more modes to manage.

  • Your home is mostly carpet or rugs rather than sealed hard flooring.
  • You want independently verified steam performance and long-term reliability before spending flagship money.
  • You dislike emptying dirty-water tanks, cleaning rollers, or maintaining multiple solution systems after use.

Price Reality Check

The Aero Ultra Steam sits firmly in the premium tier. Dreame lists it around $799.99, with early launch pricing closer to $680 and occasional promotions reported near $600.

At $600, the feature stack feels adventurous. Near $800, it needs to be genuinely excellent.

The value case depends on whether it can replace a vacuum-mop, a separate steam mop, and some manual spot cleaning. That is plausible on paper. Until independent testing confirms cleaning quality, maneuverability, drying, and upkeep, the discount is part of the recommendation—not a footnote.

Deep Dive

Four cleaning approaches in one body

Dreame combines standard wet-dry cleaning with claimed 194°F hot-water washing, 392°F steam, and a foam mode intended for stubborn or greasy messes. For the right home, that means fewer machines stored in a closet and less switching between tools.

The appeal is not raw power. It is choosing the right kind of clean without changing appliances.

  • Hot water may help loosen everyday grease and dried food.
  • Steam is positioned for more intensive hard-floor treatment.
  • FoamWash adds another option for concentrated messes.

Buyer takeaway: The versatility is compelling, but temperature and mode names do not substitute for real stain-removal tests.

Low-profile reach and claimed cordless endurance

The body is designed to lie nearly flat, with a claimed profile of about 3.88 inches, so it should reach beneath more beds, sofas, and cabinets than an upright-only washer. Dreame also claims up to 100 minutes in Eco mode and coverage up to 7,534 square feet.

Those numbers sound generous; real homes will reveal how quickly heat, suction, and dirty floors reduce them.

  • Lie-flat access could reduce the need to move heavy furniture.
  • Dirt sensing may adjust cleaning effort as conditions change.
  • Steam and maximum-power use will likely be more demanding than Eco mode.

Buyer takeaway: Buy for the layout and cleaning workflow—not for the maximum runtime claim alone.

Real-World Ownership Check

The Aero Ultra Steam is styled like an effortless appliance, but every wet-dry vacuum creates a small after-cleaning routine. Dirty water, hair, food residue, cleaning solution, and a damp roller still have to go somewhere. Dreame's hot-water self-wash and hot-air drying could make that routine more pleasant, but their effectiveness and odor control need time to prove themselves.

  • Setup: Charge the unit, fill the correct tanks, choose the appropriate mode, and confirm the flooring can tolerate steam or hot water.
  • Maintenance: Empty the dirty-water tank promptly, inspect the roller and channels, replenish solution, and allow the dock cycle to finish.
  • Fit: Plan permanent floor space for the dock and enough clearance to remove tanks without bumping cabinets.
  • Deal breaker: A complex cleaning system becomes frustrating quickly if rollers, filters, solution, or replacement parts are difficult to obtain.
